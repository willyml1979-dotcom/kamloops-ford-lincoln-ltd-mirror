2026 FORD MAVERICK FOR SALE IN NORTH VANCOUVER

Want pickup-truck versatility without a full-size footprint? The 2026 Ford Maverick is a practical compact truck for commuting through Vancouver, weekend projects and road trips around British Columbia. Choose an efficient full hybrid or an available turbocharged EcoBoost configuration that fits how you drive.

2026 MAVERICK HIGHLIGHTS

• 2.5L full hybrid powertrain available on XL, XLT and Lariat models; no external charging needed.

• Available 2.0L EcoBoost turbocharged engine for responsive performance.

• Available all-wheel drive, including eligible hybrid configurations.

• Up to 4,000 lb maximum available towing with the properly equipped 4K Tow Package; towing capacity varies.

• FLEXBED storage system and versatile cargo management.

• Standard 13.2-inch centre display with SYNC 4.

• Five-passenger SuperCrew cabin with smart storage solutions.

• Available Ford Co-Pilot360 driver-assistance technology.

• Maverick Lobo brings street-focused style, while Tremor targets more demanding off-road use.

WHY CHOOSE A MAVERICK?

The Maverick makes everyday errands and city parking easier while giving you a useful pickup bed for bikes, supplies and outdoor gear.
